03 / A journey through the mind

My orbit, in motion.

Eight stops, eight different kinds of curiosity. This is how my interests connect — from understanding people to inventing entire worlds.

STOP 01 · THE INNER UNIVERSE

Psychology — decoding the human mind

I’m interested in why people think, feel and behave the way they do. Psychology opens a window into emotions, personality, decisions and the patterns that make every person different.

Curiosity: what makes us who we are?
STOP 02 · BEYOND EARTH

Space & astronomy — looking outward

Stars, galaxies, nebulae and black holes make the universe feel wonderfully enormous. Astronomy lets me explore questions about what exists beyond Earth and how much is still waiting to be discovered.

Destination: the unknown
STOP 03 · THE SHADOW REALM

Dark fantasy — finding beauty in mystery

I’m drawn to imagined worlds filled with ancient legends, mysterious places, strange creatures and difficult choices. Dark fantasy creates an atmosphere where wonder and danger can exist side by side.

Atmosphere: mysterious · magical · cinematic
STOP 04 · THE CREATION ENGINE

Fiction writing — building worlds of my own

Writing fiction is my favourite hobby. I enjoy creating characters, shaping settings, developing conflicts and turning scattered ideas into stories. It’s where imagination becomes something I can actually share.

Main hobby: writing fiction
STOP 05 · THE STRATEGY NEBULA

Chess & strategy — thinking several moves ahead

Strategic games appeal to the part of me that enjoys patterns, planning and weighing possibilities. A good strategy is more than a quick move — it’s seeing how one decision can change what comes next.

Focus: patience · patterns · planning
STOP 06 · DIGITAL GALAXIES

Video games — exploring interactive worlds

Games combine exploration, challenges, atmosphere and storytelling. I like the feeling of stepping into a designed world and discovering how its systems, characters and choices fit together.

Mode: explore / discover / play
STOP 07 · THE DEBATE CONSTELLATION

Politics — questioning ideas and systems

I’m interested in controversial political questions and the different ideas people have about society and power. Looking at more than one perspective makes it easier to understand why debates become complicated.

Approach: question · compare · think critically
STOP 08 · THE HISTORY ARCHIVE

War history — learning from the past

Sometimes I study wars as historical events: the decisions leaders made, the societies involved, the strategies used and the consequences for ordinary people. Context matters as much as the events themselves.

Lens: history · decisions · consequences
